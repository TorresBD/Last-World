## Architecture exercised

One CPU integer20Hz clock authorizes combat, actual travel, finite construction and river steps. Ecology/engineering run on due20-tick intervals, independent of rendered frames. Offscreen populations continue processing. GPU campaign buffers are display copies; the older GPU movement benchmark remains an explicitly separate workload.

Terrain, water, soil, flora, cohorts and material ledgers use flat arrays or bounded sparse records. There is no tile/tree entity population and no second renderer. Current resident terrain is4.8×3.2km with8m samples,16m navigation and32m commander masks; this is not the production fine-page/streaming allocation policy. All10,000military and2,000workers remain in the normal game. The CPU connected fixture explicitly uses512/64 for correctness; no test or rendering cap silently reduces simulated stocks.

Shared navigation keeps partial work across irrelevant cost changes, checks live passability, and refreshes completed cost fields beside their live predecessors within the same4,096-node/tick expansion budget. Search/frontier/deadline state survives saves. Route lanes and bounded, collision-checked friendly yielding keep parked units from trapping workers. Both the earlier core and current-source bounded regular scenarios exercise these mechanisms; ordinary10,000+2,000 integrated behavior and actual PC performance remain separate pending gates.

Physical engineering spends finite biomass/substrate and elapsed awake work. Bent passages, actual room/nest capacity, supported canopy/cavities, pits/wallows, dams, reefs and soil mixing persist independently of their builders. Same-cell approaches require physical travel; stalled optional jobs release slots with saved cooldowns and keep their animals/material wallets. Actual living roots/cover/shade and mixed-soil porosity project into ground rates without creating water or soil. Local craters and erosion affect actual intersecting roofs; canopy loss exposes real occupants and clutch references.

The surface-water graph conserves named liters and staged fluxes. Its dynamic region remains local; a dam elsewhere is rejected. Underground flooding is an entrance-connected hazard projection, without a second invented water volume. Momentum, complete catchments/aquifers and production-world hydraulic streaming are not proven here.

Capture uses a real animal identity, actual handler travel and temperament-dependent handling time, followed by physical pen delivery. An existing materialized individual keeps its identity; custody suspends its former physical work without deleting finite owned material. Care consumes faction food/water; research and breeding require actual housed specimens, compatible reproduction and finite capacity. Release orders travel to the clicked location and move an existing animal there. Exact ledgers, delayed development, atomic saves and retained previous saves are independent acceptance conditions. The prior core and current-source bounded regular scenarios completed that lifecycle/late save; current mortality fixtures still pass. No rescue spawning or population floors are used.
